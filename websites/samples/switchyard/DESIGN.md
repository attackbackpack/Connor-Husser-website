# Switchyard Cycle concept system

This fictional sample translates a velodrome lap board and repair-bench service
tags into a local bicycle-repair website. Track blue carries the main field;
workshop yellow marks the selected symptom; asphalt panels
hold inactive states; chalk white carries type. A narrow red lane rule separates
the opening interaction from the rest of the page. The hero uses one generated,
text-free tactile background of painted track aggregate, enamel plates, brushed
steel, hooks, fasteners, and a mechanical counter. All meaningful copy and
controls remain semantic HTML over that material layer.

Barlow Condensed is the display face and Barlow is the reading face. Geometry
is primarily square, lines are mechanical, and numeric slots use tabular
figures. Small radii belong only to physical enamel plates; restrained shadows
describe their real depth. The page has no generic cards, invented contact
details, pricing, testimonials, or outcomes.

The signature interaction keeps three symptom slots fixed while the selected
slot and screen-reader recommendation change instantly. It is a starting-point
explainer, not a browser diagnosis. Mobile stacks the fixed slots vertically
and preserves the same order and labels.
