The board finds a starting point; it does not pretend to diagnose a bike from a browser. The real work begins with inspection.

01

Quick check

A focused look at one symptom, followed by a plain-language explanation of what was found and what should happen next.

02

Tune-up

A broader adjustment pass for shifting, braking, tire pressure, and the small issues that make a bike feel harder to ride.

03

Deep service

A fuller inspection and service plan when wear, damage, or several interacting problems need more than an adjustment.
