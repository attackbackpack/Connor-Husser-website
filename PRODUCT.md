# Product — Connor Husser

## One identity, two routes

Connor Husser is one personal identity, not an agency. The root route is an
artifact-led portfolio for college reviewers, collaborators, and general
visitors. `/websites/` is a service route for local-business owners. They are
different entry points into the same work, not separate brands.

The root portfolio is the canonical college-application link. A client footer,
when one is appropriate and approved, should point to `/websites/`.

## What the work is for

Connor designs websites, tools, and systems that make complicated things
clearer. The portfolio records the problem, role, decisions, built artifact,
and status. The service route explains a direct sequence: Presence Check →
Website Build → Owner Handoff. Connor personally reviews, designs, builds, and
explains the handoff; this is a personal practice, not a multi-person agency.
