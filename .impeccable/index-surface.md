# Index surface brief

## Scope and mode

- Target: `index.html`
- Mode: Persuade
- Audience: owners of local businesses who want a credible online presence without managing a web project themselves.
- Job: understand Connor's offer, trust the honesty of his current experience, and start a direct conversation.
- Primary action: show Connor the business for a presence check and project-fit conversation.

## Approved direction

- World: Seeded Identity, a parametric mark whose seed drives its line field, palette, dividers, controls, and motion together.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/transformation-field.webp`
- Memorable moment: Clarity, Proof, and Contact enter from the left as separate lines, converge into one living field, and resolve into “Your real business, made clear online.”
- Plain language and honest evidence keep the identity from reading like a technology product.

## Content and evidence

- Lead with the outcome, not AI or production speed.
- Offer sequence: Presence Check, Website Build, Owner Handoff.
- Work index must distinguish a real family project from unsolicited Hawaii concept studies.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, client counts, metrics, performance claims, ranking promises, or prices.
- Existing contact details are unverified placeholders and must remain visibly identified in source for replacement.

## Component grammar

- Luminous off-white ground with deep night ink; violet, azure, mint, and yellow are seed roles rather than decorative accents.
- Flat vector surfaces, no decorative shadows, glass, or generic cards.
- One-pixel rules and hairline canvas strokes; pills are reserved for compact actions and range controls.
- Corners remain mostly square; the living field supplies organic curvature.
- Motion is one orchestrated transformation: inputs perturb the field and page accents in lockstep. Reduced motion receives an equally complete static seed.

## Responsive contract

- Desktop preserves the left-input / center-field / right-outcome topology.
- On phones the headline and action lead, the field becomes a shorter full-width band, and the three controls stack below it without horizontal overflow.
- Service rows retain their sequence and labels; work status never relies on color alone.
- Keyboard focus, enlarged text, and reduced motion remain first-class.
